📱 iPhone

Shoot in 48 MP #18

Photo Mode is set to 24 MP out of the box — half the resolution you paid for.

Settings › Camera › Formats › Resolution Control

Grid + Level #22

Both off by default; the level line turns yellow when your shot is straight.

Settings › Camera › Grid + Level

Un-mirror your selfies #27

The preview is mirrored, the saved photo is flipped back — turn this on so you get what you saw.

Settings › Camera › Composition › Mirror Front Camera

Clean Up strangers from photos #19

Remove photobombers instead of cropping. Apple Intelligence, iPhone 15 Pro and newer.

Photos › Edit › Tools › Clean Up

Screen unknown callers #13

Your iPhone can ask unknown callers why they're calling before it even rings.

Settings › Apps › Phone › Screen Unknown Callers › Ask Reason for Calling

Adaptive Power #33

The smarter battery mode is off by default on iPhone 15 Pro/16 — it needs about a week to learn you.

Settings › Battery › Power Mode › Adaptive Power

Record + summarize calls #31

Both sides hear a notice; the AI summary lands in Notes. iPhone 15 Pro+, not in the EU.

During a call: More › Call Recording — summary in Notes › Call Recordings

Live-translate phone calls #39

Download the language first; both sides are notified. Apple Intelligence, not in the EU.

During a call: More › Live Translation › pick language › Start

Screenshot → calendar event #35

Screenshot a flyer or ticket and let Visual Intelligence put it in your calendar.

Side button + Volume up › Add to Calendar › Create Event

Try the new Siri #37

Siri AI on iOS 27 is opt-in — you keep the old Siri until you flip this.

Settings › Siri › Try Siri AI (Beta)

Significant Locations #6

Your iPhone quietly keeps a list of the places you visit — check it and decide for yourself.

iCloud Advanced Data Protection #12

By default Apple keeps the keys to your iCloud backup. This setting hands them to you.

🎧 AirPods & Apple Watch

AirPods Hearing Test #32

A real ~5-minute hearing test with AirPods Pro 2/3 — result per ear, exportable as PDF.

Settings › your AirPods › Hearing Health › Take a Hearing Test

AirPods Live Translation #16

Hold both stems and your AirPods translate 9 languages. Pro 2+ / AirPods 4 ANC + iPhone 15 Pro+.

Settings › your AirPods › Translation › Languages

Fall Detection: Always on #30

Under 55 it often only works during workouts — especially on upgraded watches.

Watch app › My Watch › Emergency SOS › Fall Detection › Always on

Workout Buddy #41

The AI coach in your ear is off until you enable it per workout type. Needs Bluetooth earbuds.

Workout › Outdoor Run › Alerts › Workout Buddy › Use Workout Buddy

🤖 Samsung & Android

Full screen resolution (QHD+) #40

Ultra and Plus Galaxys ship at FHD+ although the panel does almost twice the pixels.

Settings › Display › Screen resolution › QHD+ › Apply

Audio Eraser #20

Galaxy AI removes wind and noise from videos you already shot. S24+ / Z series, One UI 7+.

Gallery › Edit › Audio › Audio eraser

Best Face #29

Group shot, one person blinking? Swap faces from the motion photo — up to 5 people.

Gallery › ⋮ › Details › Choose best face

Circle to Search #23

Hold the navigation handle and circle anything on screen to find it. Pixel 6+ and many Galaxys.

Hold nav handle / Home › circle it (Samsung: Settings › Display › Navigation bar)

Theft Detection Lock #15

Your phone can lock itself when it detects a snatch — on by default only in Brazil.

📺 TV & Gaming

Filmmaker Mode #34

One picture mode that kills motion smoothing, sharpening and noise reduction at once. Looks darker at first — that's correct.

Settings › All Settings › Picture › Picture Mode › Filmmaker Mode

Sharpness to 0 #36

"Sharpness" is edge enhancement: halos, no extra detail. Zero is the honest picture.

Settings › All Settings › Picture › Expert Settings › Sharpness → 0

PS5 in the right HDMI port #38

Many TVs do 4K 120 on only 1–2 inputs (on many Samsungs it's HDMI 4 with the controller icon).

PS5: Settings › Screen and Video › Video Output › 120 Hz Output

Smart TV ACR tracking #3

Your TV can fingerprint everything you watch and sell it on — switch the "viewing data" options off.

🏠 Smart Home

Ring: kick out old phones #26

A lost or sold phone that's still signed in keeps seeing your cameras.

Ring app › Menu › Control Center › Authorized Client Devices › Remove All Devices

Ring: audio recording off #28

Your doorbell records sound with every motion clip — voices up to 25 ft away.

Menu › Devices › your doorbell › Device Settings › Privacy Settings › Audio Streaming and Recording

Ring Search Party #8

Your camera joins AI lost-dog searches by default — decide if you want that.

Amazon Sidewalk #11

Your Echo shares a slice of your internet with the neighborhood unless you switch it off.

🧠 Apps & AI

WhatsApp two-step PIN #17

The "sent you my code by mistake" takeover dies here: set the 6-digit PIN + recovery email.

WhatsApp › Settings › Account › Two-step verification

SIM PIN #9

A stolen SIM in another phone receives your bank codes — unless it asks for a PIN.

Chrome Enhanced protection #10

Safe Browsing runs on "Standard" by default; Enhanced catches more phishing.

Gemini: Keep Activity #14

On by default for adults — reviewed chats can be kept for up to 3 years. Your call.

Gemini Live camera #25

Point your camera at anything and ask — free on Android and iPhone.

Gemini app › Live › camera button

Google Maps offline #21

Driving directions with zero signal — a small area is under 100 MB.

Profile picture › Offline maps › Select your own map › Download

Spotify Lossless #24

Premium includes lossless, but it's off until you pick it per device. Not over Bluetooth.

Profile picture › Settings and privacy › Media quality › Lossless
